# 1. Feature Overview

The AI Receptionist & WhatsApp Front Desk is one of the most commercially valuable features in ClinicOS.

For many clinics, this feature alone can justify purchasing the platform.

Most clinics lose patients because:

Nobody answers after hours.

Receptionists are busy.

Calls get missed.

WhatsApp messages go unanswered.

Patients never book appointments.

The AI Receptionist becomes a 24/7 digital front desk that can:

Answer questions

Book appointments

Reschedule visits

Share clinic information

Collect patient details

Handle follow-ups

Answer FAQs

Escalate to humans

all through WhatsApp and future channels.

The AI Receptionist & WhatsApp Front Desk acts as a 24/7 digital patient-facing assistant that automates appointment booking, inquiries, lead capture, follow-ups, clinic information, patient communication, and front-desk workflows while intelligently escalating complex conversations to humans, helping clinics improve acquisition, retention, operational efficiency, and patient experience.
