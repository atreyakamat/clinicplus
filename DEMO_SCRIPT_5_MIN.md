# 5-Minute ClinicOS Demo Script

## Objective
Demonstrate core ClinicOS functionality in a quick, impactful overview suitable for initial clinic introductions.

## Target Audience
Clinic administrators, doctors, and decision-makers evaluating ClinicOS for their practice.

## Demo Flow (5 Minutes Total)

### 0:00-0:30 - Introduction & Login (30 seconds)
- "Welcome to ClinicOS - the operating system for modern clinics"
- Quick login using demo credentials:
  - Email: doctor.demo@clinicos.com
  - Password: DemoPass123!
- "You're now logged in as Dr. Demo Doctor at our flagship clinic"

### 0:30-1:30 - Dashboard Overview (1 minute)
- Show main dashboard with key metrics:
  - Today's appointments: [number]
  - Pending follow-ups: [number]
  - Today's revenue: [amount]
  - Active patients: [number]
- Highlight: Real-time updates, color-coded status indicators
- "All your clinic's vital signs at a glance"

### 1:30-2:30 - Patient Registration & Lookup (1 minute)
- Click "Patients" → "New Patient"
- Demonstrate quick patient registration:
  - Patient Code: Auto-generated PT-XXXXXX
  - First Name: [Demo]
  - Last Name: [Patient]
  - DOB, contact info, etc.
- Show patient lookup/search functionality
- "Register new patients in under 30 seconds"

### 2:30-3:30 - Appointment Booking (1 minute)
- Click "Appointments" → "New Appointment"
- Select patient from lookup
- Choose doctor, date/time (show calendar view)
- Set appointment type, duration
- Confirm booking
- "Schedule appointments with intelligent conflict detection"

### 3:30-4:30 - Consultation & Checkout (1 minute)
- Show completed appointment → "Start Consultation"
- Demonstrate consultation notes:
  - Chief complaint
  - Clinical assessment
  - Treatment plan
  - Vital signs entry
  - Diagnosis selection
- Show prescription generation
- Click "Checkout" → Generate invoice → Process payment (CASH/UPI/CARD)
- "Complete patient visits with integrated billing"

### 4:30-5:00 - Closing & Key Benefits (30 seconds)
- "In just 5 minutes, we've shown:"
  - Patient registration
  - Appointment scheduling
  - Clinical documentation
  - Prescription & billing
- "Plus: Automated reminders, secure messaging, and comprehensive reporting"
- "Ready to transform your clinic? Let's talk implementation."
